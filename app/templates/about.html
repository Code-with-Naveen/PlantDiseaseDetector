About this project

The problem

Crop diseases cause major yield losses, and early identification is hard without an expert. This app lets anyone photograph a leaf and instantly get a likely diagnosis, a heatmap of what the AI focused on, and practical advice.

How it works

  1. You upload a leaf photo; it is validated and resized.
  2. A ResNet9 convolutional neural network (PyTorch) classifies it into one of {{ crops.values()|map('length')|sum }} classes.
  3. Grad-CAM highlights the regions that influenced the decision.
  4. Results are stored in a local SQLite database and shown in History and Dashboard.

Tips for best results

  • Photograph a single leaf, filling most of the frame.
  • Use good natural light, avoid heavy shadows and blur.
  • A plain background works best — the training images have one.
Limitations. The model only knows the crops listed here, was trained on studio-style photos, and can be over-confident on unrelated images. Treat results as guidance, not a laboratory diagnosis.
